Welcome to MaryOS

This is Liquid Platinum, the MaryOS desktop. A few things to try:

  Finder      Double-click a folder to open it, a text file to edit it.
              ⌘↑ goes up a folder, ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward.
              Return renames, ⇧⌘N makes a folder, ⌘⌫ moves to the Trash.
              Drag files onto folders, other windows, or the Trash.
              Right-click for the rest.

  TextEdit    Edit this note and press ⌘S: the Finder sees the change at once.

  Spotlight   Ctrl+Space (or ⌘+Space) searches your apps and windows.

  Terminal    ⌘T opens one.

The ⌘ key is the Super (Windows) key on your keyboard.
